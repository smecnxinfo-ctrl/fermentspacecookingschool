Yes — most of our guests are beginners. Start gently: a short sauna session, a quick cool-down, then a brief dip in the ice bath, and repeat a couple of rounds. Drink plenty of water and listen to your body. If you are pregnant or have a heart condition, high blood pressure or another medical concern, please check with your doctor before using the sauna or ice bath.

A common starting point is 10–15 minutes in the sauna and 30 seconds to 2 minutes in the ice bath, repeated 2–3 times. Build up slowly over several visits and get out any time you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.
